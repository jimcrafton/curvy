Booleans and masks

Select two or more shapes (click, Shift-click or marquee) and tap Space for the Combine ring: Union, Subtract, Intersect, Exclude make a live Boolean node (drag an operand and the result follows), Divide and Shape builder (B: drag across regions to merge them, Alt-drag to delete them) make plain shapes. Clip and Opacity mask use the top-most selected shape as the mask. Double-click a node to enter it; each operand has a mode (add, subtract, intersect, exclude), an eye and an order in the HUD. Hold R or S and move up and down to rotate or scale. Try: both circles, Subtract; Stripes + Disc, Opacity mask; Disc fill to a gradient inside the mask.

How it works (proposal)

  • A boolean is a node with ordered operands. The Boolean node has any number of inputs; the first is the base and every later input carries a mode: add, subtract, intersect or exclude, folded in order. Union is all adds; Subtract is the bottom shape with every other input subtracting (Illustrator's "Minus Front"); "Reverse order" makes the top shape the base ("Minus Back"). Nothing is destroyed: operands keep their own geometry and transforms, hidden operands (the eye) are skipped, and the result is an ordinary path with the node's own fill and stroke (copied from the base at creation).
  • Live editing. Enter the node (double-click, E from the ring) to see every operand as an outlined ghost coloured by its mode (blue add, red subtract, green intersect, purple exclude); move or rotate one and the result recomputes. The ghosts also show, as thin outlines, whenever the node is merely selected. Booleans of booleans work because a node's geometry is just a function of its inputs.
  • Separate and Expand. Separate puts the operands back as separate shapes with their own appearance (an opacity mask's shape gets its original fill back, because the gradient belonged to its role as a mask; position and size edits stay). Expand bakes the result into a plain shape (a compound path with holes drawn with the even-odd rule) and forgets the operands.
  • Shape builder and Divide are destructive. They cut the selection into the regions of its overlap (up to six shapes), then keep each as a plain shape: Divide keeps all regions, the builder lets you drag across regions to merge them (one new shape) or Alt-drag to delete them. They are commands, not nodes, because their output has no meaningful live inputs. Appearance of a region comes from the top-most shape that covers it.
  • Clip mask. A Clip node has Content inputs and one Mask input; the result is the content clipped to the mask shape (rendered as an SVG clipPath, so strokes, gradients and nested nodes clip correctly, and no geometry is computed). Entering shows the unclipped content faded, with the mask shape outlined in orange. The mask can be any shape or Boolean node.
  • Opacity mask. Same structure, but the mask is a luminosity mask: white shows, black hides, greys fade. Give the mask shape a white-to-black gradient for a soft fade; Invert flips it. It maps to an SVG mask. In the future a mask input could also be any node that renders, such as a blurred shape.
  • Blend and opacity. Every node has an opacity and a blend mode (normal, multiply, screen, overlay, darken, lighten, difference, soft light) in the HUD and a Blend sub-ring. They compose with masks (the mask applies first).
  • Rings. Two or more selected: Union, Subtract, Intersect, Exclude, Divide, Clip, Opacity mask, Shape builder. One shape: Duplicate, Delete, Front, Back, Blend…, Hide, Lock. One Boolean or mask node: Enter, Separate, Expand (Boolean only), Reverse (Boolean) or Invert (opacity mask), Duplicate, Delete, Blend…, Hide. An operand while inside a Boolean: Add, Subtract, Intersect, Exclude, Hide, Duplicate, Delete, Exit. Empty canvas: Select all, Invert, Deselect, Exit node, Operands on/off, Undo, Reset, and more.
  • Geometry in the mock. Shapes are flattened polygons and the booleans use the polygon-clipping library (MIT, in tools/). The real editor needs the same ops on curves: either exact bezier clipping (as Paper.js does) or flatten, clip, then refit with fitCurve (lossy; fine for a first version). Strokes and open paths cannot take part until outlined (an Outline stroke command, not mocked). jitnodes is polyline-only today, so a first boolean node can run on flattened polylines.
  • Not mocked: compound paths made by hand (Make compound), Outline stroke, knockout groups and isolation, gradient mask handles on canvas, trim/merge/crop pathfinder variants (reachable with operand modes), boolean of text, direct editing of an Expanded result's anchors (that is the pen bench), full transform handles (the transform bench).
