Curvy: the workflow, top to bottom

One pass through the whole editor, from an empty window to exported files. Each step shows the screen at that moment, what the pen and keyboard do, and what the document does underneath. The pictures are storyboards drawn from the design in docs/ui-design.md; every step links to the interactive bench where that part can be tried. The same few surfaces appear again and again: the canvas, the one-line hint bar, the radial ring (tap Space or press the pen's barrel button), the translucent HUD card, and hold-a-key scrubbing.
