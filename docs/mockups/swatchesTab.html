Swatches tab of the document card

  • Same palettes as the colour card's strip. Both are views of one list (one controller), so a swatch added, renamed or reordered in one shows in the other at once. Every change is one undo step.
  • Palettes are rows, each with its colours as a strip: click one to make it active (it is outlined and opens below). The … button has New, Duplicate, Rename, Delete and New palette from image. Recent is the last row, read-only.
  • Grid: click selects and shows the details; right-click (or hold with a pen) Use, Replace with current colour, Rename, Delete; drag reorders; + adds the current colour. A white corner mark is a linked swatch.
  • Details: name, hex, Linked, and how many shapes in how many groups use it. Select them selects those shapes on the canvas. Typing a hex on a linked swatch recolours every user at once.
  • In use, in no palette: every colour found on a colour row that no swatch matches, with its count. + adds it to the active palette. Worked out when the tab opens, not kept up to date while you draw.

New palette from image

  • Pick an image file (PNG or JPG). It is shrunk to about 128 px on its long side and the colours are found by median cut: the pixels are split along the widest colour channel until there are as many boxes as colours asked for, and each box's average becomes a swatch.
  • Colours 4–16 (default 8). Order by most used, hue or lightness. Merge near-duplicates joins swatches closer than a small distance. Ignore near-white and near-black leaves out paper and shadow.
  • The swatches update as the controls move. Create palette makes plain (unlinked) swatches named by hex, a new active palette named after the file; one undo step. Cancel changes nothing.
  • The three sample pictures stand in for the file dialog.
