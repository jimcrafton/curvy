Swatches in the colour card

  • One active palette sits under the sliders. The name opens the list of palettes; the … button has New, Duplicate, Rename and Delete.
  • Click a swatch to use its colour: the wheel, sliders and the fill's colour follow (one undo step). The outlined swatch is the one that matches the current colour.
  • + saves the colour on show into the active palette. Hovering a swatch shows its name and hex below the grid.
  • Right-click (or hold, with a pen) a swatch: Use, Replace with current colour, Rename, Delete. Drag a swatch to reorder.
  • Recent is automatic: the last colours you applied, newest first. Read-only, no +, cannot be renamed or deleted. Keys 1–8 pick from it. Right-click a recent colour and Save to palette moves it into the active palette.
  • Deleting a palette asks first and can be undone with Undo; a document always keeps at least one palette.
  • Palettes are saved in the document (names, colour order). Import of .ASE / .ACO and "palette from image" come later.
  • Plain swatches are a copy of the colour: editing a swatch does not recolour shapes. Linked (global) swatches are a later step.
