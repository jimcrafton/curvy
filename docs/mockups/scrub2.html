Context scrubbing

Live. Move the mouse over the objects: the part under the cursor is outlined and decides which keys scrub what. Hold a key and move sideways; Shift snaps; release keeps the value. The control box stays where the key was pressed and only its value moves.

  • Try: the ellipse outline, inside the ellipse, the gradient rectangle (outline and inside), the gaps between the letters of the word, and the letters themselves. Each gives different keys. Click the canvas first so it has the keyboard.
  • Fixed control. The slider or nib is placed when the key goes down and does not follow the pointer; the pointer's sideways distance from there is the value. The small + is the real pointer.
  • Hints. Full names the context and each key. Keys only shows just keycaps with a small icon, no words. Off shows nothing until a key is held (the value must still show). In the product this is a setting, and hints also retire by themselves with use.
  • Keys are placeholders. The same letter keeps its meaning where it can (W size, O opacity, H hue), and context-specific ones (A angle, K spacing, Y height, B baseline, L lightness) are remappable.
