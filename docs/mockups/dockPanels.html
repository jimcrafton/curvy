The dock

  • Dock class: a column holding zero or more Panels. The right dock is the panel dock; the left dock holds the toolbar and the bottom dock the timeline; the top dock is reserved and empty. Each is a Dock with the same class, so any panel could live in any dock later.
  • No dock scrollbar. When the panels together are taller than the dock, scroll the dock with the mouse wheel (not while a panel is being dragged). Over a panel's content the wheel scrolls that panel first and hands over to the dock only when the panel is at its end; over a title or a gap it scrolls the dock. A faint position hint shows for 0.7 s while the dock scrolls.
  • Panels may have their own scrollbar (Document, Layers and Properties here), thin and only on the panel, so the dock itself stays clean.

The Panel class

  • A small title area (20 px): a grip, a collapse caret, the title, and a menu and a hide button that appear on hover. Content below is scrollable. A panel has canTearOff.
  • Dragging the title: inside the dock it reorders (an amber line shows the drop place). Drag past the tear-off distance (40 px outside the dock) and the panel becomes a floating newui::ToolFrame hosting it, and the same drag carries on moving the frame.
  • Docking again: drag the frame by its title over the dock (it outlines in amber) and release. Closing the frame docks the panel back into the right dock; Hide (in the panel menu or the Panels button) is how a panel is put away.
  • canTearOff is true only for the panels of the right dock. The toolbar (left dock) and the timeline (bottom dock) have it false: they stay where they are and cannot be reordered.

What the panels are

  • Document is the document card as a panel: tabs Document | Swatches | Library | Fonts inside it (a TabControl in the panel body), so the card is no longer a separate floating thing. Tear it off and it is the floating card of the earlier mockup.
  • Colour, Appearance, Transform, Layers, Properties are what the old Panels button showed. The Panels button now lists every panel with a tick for shown ones; clicking toggles it. A hidden panel returns at the bottom of the dock.
