Scrubbing properties, ZBrush style

This one is live. Move the mouse over the canvas, then hold W, O or H and move sideways to scrub width, opacity or hue of the two shapes; Shift snaps to preset values; [ and ] step the width. Release the key to keep the value. Each variation shows the same gesture differently.

Hand
  • Click the canvas first so it has the keyboard, then hold a key and move the mouse.

    • What this borrows from ZBrush

  • Key pops a slider at the cursor. In ZBrush a hotkey on a slider (S for Draw Size, U for Z Intensity, O for Focal Shift) shows it under the cursor, so nothing is hunted for in a panel. (1)
  • Hold and sweep. Community plugins add hold-a-key-and-move-sideways, with the value shown live and the old tool restored on release. (2, 4)
  • The cursor is the readout. ZBrush's brush circle is the draw size and the inner ring the focal shift. For us the nib circle is the stroke width. (3)
  • Shift snaps, bracket keys step. ZBrush snaps Draw Size to whole numbers and Focal Shift to -100, 0, 100 with Shift; Draw Size has increment keys. (5)
  • Not copied: ZBrush uses S for size. Here S is Star, so width is W. Every letter can be re-bound, with conflicts replaced as ZBrush does.
  • Each tool remembers its own values, as ZBrush keeps separate sizes for painting and sculpting. Nothing here shows that, but the real app should.
